Each physical mat block provides 3-in-1 Multi-Options (e.g. [ A | N | 0 ] on GPIO 32, [ B | O | 1 ] on GPIO 33, [ C | P | 2 ] on VP Pin / GPIO 36, [ D | Q | 3 ] on GPIO 34, [ E | R | 4 ] on GPIO 35). Step on your physical piezo mat or click any letter or number below to launch 3D models or select curriculum.

⚡ Physical Tactile Sensor Mat Listener Active (Letters A, B, C, D, E Integrated)
Stepping on GPIO 32 triggers Block 1 (A), GPIO 33 triggers Block 2 (B), VP Pin (GPIO 36) triggers Block 3 (C), GPIO 34 triggers Block 4 (D), and GPIO 35 triggers Block 5 (E). Press keys A–D on your keyboard to emulate steps.
