AlphaMat™

Smart Tactile Sensor Mat & 3D Interactive Learning Studio

⚡ Piezo Sensors • 🕶️ 3D AR Models • 🔊 Audio Narration

A tactile beginning for a digital generation.

AlphaMat bridges the best of both worlds: a beautifully responsive physical mat that invites movement and tactile curiosity, paired with a rich 3D digital companion that brings every discovery to life. No passive video screens—just active physical exploration.

How It Works

One touch. A world of learning.

Seamless hardware-to-digital communication brings instant delight to children and students.

01

Touch

A child steps or presses on an alphabet pad on the tactile piezo sensor mat.

02

Detect

High-sensitivity piezoelectric sensors and ESP32 microcontroller convert touch pressure into real-time telemetry.

03

Explore

The studio instantly responds with 360° interactive 3D models, audio speech narration, and curriculum fun facts.

The AlphaMat Advantage

Designed for little hands. Built for big imaginations.

Carefully engineered to replace passive screen time with active, multi-sensory STEM discovery.

🌱

Learning That Grows

No boring drills or high pressure. Children discover phonics, animals, fruits, and ecosystems through self-directed play.

📡

Responsive by Design

Physical actions have an immediate digital echo, helping kids understand cause-and-effect and building motor memory.

👓

See Every Angle in 3D & AR

Rotate 3D models 360°, inspect animal anatomy, and project creatures into your living room or classroom with WebXR AR.

Technology

Quietly clever under the surface.

Reliable hardware sensor telemetry and instant cloud sync built for seamless classrooms.

⚡

ESP32 & Piezo Array

The Signal: Low-latency physical matrix sensing and ADC pulse processing.

☁️

Firebase Cloud Sync

The Bridge: Real-time data keys synchronizing hardware steps with the app.

🥽

3D WebXR Studio

The Response: Interactive 3D models, audio narration, and fun facts.
